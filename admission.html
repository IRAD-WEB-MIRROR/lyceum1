Admission 2027

Entrance test for grades 8 and 10

Registration: 1 March – 5 April 2027. Bring the application form, the pupil's birth certificate or passport, the report card for the last year and two photographs 3×4. Pupils from the districts can register by post through their school.
GradePlacesEntrance test
850 (two classes)Sunday 18 April 2027, 9:00: mathematics (2 hours) and physics (1 hour)
1025 (one class)Sunday 25 April 2027, 9:00: mathematics and physics (2 hours each)

The test is written in Iradi or in English, as the pupil chooses. Results are posted at the Lyceum and on this site by 10 May. Winners of the ISEZ school olympiad in mathematics or physics (1st and 2nd places) are admitted without the test.

Preparation

Sunday preparation course, October to April, at the Lyceum: free for pupils of district schools, 400 som a month for city pupils. Sample tests of earlier years are given at the Lyceum office (room 104).

Cost

Teaching is free (state lyceum). Boarding house: free for pupils from the districts whose families have a low income; otherwise 1,200 som a month with meals. The uniform is the Lyceum blazer with the school badge.
