Sample entrance test

Grade 8 · mathematics and physics · questions from the papers of earlier years

Twelve questions like those of the real test for grade 8. The real test has 20 questions in mathematics (2 hours) and 10 in physics (1 hour), and you show your working. Here you write only the answer. Use a pen and paper, no calculator.

  1. 3/4 + 5/6 − 1/3 = ?

    9/12 + 10/12 − 4/12 = 15/12 = 5/4.

  2. Solve: 3(x − 2) = 2x + 5.   x = ?

    3x − 6 = 2x + 5, so x = 11.

  3. A train covers 180 km in 2 hours 15 minutes. What is its average speed in km/h?

    2 h 15 min = 2.25 h; 180 / 2.25 = 80.

  4. The length of a rectangle is three times its width, and its perimeter is 48 cm. Find its area in cm².

    2(3w + w) = 48, w = 6, length 18; 6 × 18 = 108.

  5. Which number is divisible by 9?

    4 + 7 + 2 + 5 = 18, which is divisible by 9.

  6. The price of a book rose by 20%, then fell by 20%. Compared with the first price it is now:

    1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96.

  7. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. How many degrees is the largest angle?

    2 + 3 + 4 = 9 parts; 180 / 9 = 20; 4 × 20 = 80.

  8. What is the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 100?

    50 pairs of 101 (1 + 100, 2 + 99, ...).

  9. In which unit is force measured?

    1 N = 1 kg·m/s².

  10. A block with a volume of 2 dm³ has a mass of 5.4 kg. What is its density in kg/m³?

    2 dm³ = 0.002 m³; 5.4 / 0.002 = 2,700 (aluminium).

  11. A clean steel needle laid gently on water does not sink. Why?

    The surface of the water acts like a thin film.

  12. At a summer pasture high in the mountains water boils at a lower temperature than in Talas, because:

    Boiling point falls as pressure falls.
