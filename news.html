News

All news since 2012

28 Oct 2026Team for the Zhautykov Olympiad chosen
Seven pupils of grades 10–12 will travel to Almaty in January for the Zhautykov International Olympiad in mathematics, physics and computer science.
12 Oct 2026Physics course for district teachers
In February the Lyceum will hold a two-week course for physics teachers of district schools who have no physics degree. Apply through your head of school.
20 Sep 2026Boarding house roof finished
The new roof of the boarding house is finished before winter, paid by the Lyceum Trust. The class of 1996 gave a third of the cost.
1 Sep 2026Welcome, class of 2031!
50 new pupils in grade 8 and 25 in grade 10. Of the new pupils 29 come from district schools and 18 live in the boarding house.
30 Jun 2026Graduation Test 2026: first again
Our 55 graduates averaged 387 points, the highest in the ISEZ for the third year running (results on e-Irad).
18 Apr 2026Entrance test: 412 candidates for 75 places
A record number of candidates wrote the entrance test. Results are posted at the Lyceum and in the admission office.
26 Jan 2026Zhautykov Olympiad: silver and bronze
Bekzat T. won a silver medal in physics and Aiperi K. a bronze in mathematics in Almaty.
11 Mar 2025Irad Day concert
The Lyceum choir and the physics teachers' band played at the Irad Day concert in the TSU assembly hall.
2 Sep 2024New computer room
The second computer room has 20 new computers, from the Lyceum Trust and Attelkom. The old ones go to Teskey-Jar and two other village schools.
29 Jun 2024Graduation 2024
52 graduates, average 379 in the Graduation Test. 12 graduates won places at universities abroad.
14 Nov 2023Lessons from 9:00 in the cold weeks
Until the end of February lessons begin at 9:00 and end at 16:10 because of the early darkness and the cold in the main building.
5 Oct 2022Our new website
The Lyceum has a new website, made by graduates of 2009 and 2014. The news of the old site from 2012 is kept here.
1 Sep 2020Back at the Lyceum
After the quarantine the Lyceum is open again for all grades. Boarders arrive on 30 August; masks in the corridors.
20 Mar 2020Lyceum closed under the quarantine
Under Executive Directive 2020/05 the Lyceum is closed from 19 March. Tasks are sent by e-mail and through the class teachers; boarders have gone home.
2 Sep 201940 years of the Lyceum
On 1 September 1979 the first 90 pupils came to the Republican boarding school. 600 graduates came for the anniversary evening.
1 Sep 2016New director
Dr Ulan Tashtanov, graduate of 1987 and lecturer at TSU, is the new director of the Lyceum.
12 Jun 2015Ten years of the Graduation Test
Since the first National Graduation Test in 2005 the Lyceum has had the highest average every year but two (2009 and 2011).
15 Oct 2012Laboratory wing opened
The new wing has laboratories of physics and chemistry and two computer rooms. Thank you to the Lyceum Trust and the graduates in Houston and Boston.
1 Feb 2012Old site
The Lyceum opened its first page on the Education Network (lyceum1.iradedu.org).

News before October 2022 was moved from the Lyceum's old page and may have lost its photographs.
